# Juniper Bay Payments Wire and EFT Fees and Cut-off Times

Owner: Head of Payments Product
Applies to: Juniper Bay Payments
Classification: Public
Version: 3.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

This schedule lists the fees and daily cut-off times for wire transfers and electronic funds transfers (EFT) offered by Juniper Bay Payments, a subsidiary of Juniper Bay Financial Group. Juniper Bay Financial Group is fictional; all figures are illustrative.

## Who this applies to

The schedule applies to business clients that hold a payments account with Juniper Bay Payments. Personal accounts at Juniper Bay Bank are covered by the Juniper Bay Bank fee disclosures, not this schedule.

## Wire transfer fees

| Service | Fee (CAD) |
|---|---|
| Outgoing domestic wire | 25 |
| Outgoing international wire | 45 |
| Incoming domestic wire | 10 |
| Incoming international wire | 15 |
| Wire recall or amendment request | 30 |
| Returned wire handling | 20 |

Correspondent and intermediary banks may deduct their own charges from international wires. Juniper Bay Payments does not control these charges and cannot refund them.

## EFT fees

| Service | Fee (CAD) |
|---|---|
| Pre-authorized debit (per item) | 0.50 |
| Direct deposit credit (per item) | 0.40 |
| Same-day EFT surcharge (per file) | 15 |
| Returned or rejected EFT item | 5 |

## Daily cut-off times

All times are Eastern Time on a Canadian business day. Instructions received after a cut-off are processed on the next business day.

| Service | Cut-off |
|---|---|
| Outgoing domestic wire (same day) | 4:00 p.m. |
| Outgoing international wire (same day) | 2:00 p.m. |
| Incoming wire credit to account | 5:00 p.m. |
| EFT file submission (next day) | 6:00 p.m. |
| EFT file submission (same day) | 11:00 a.m. |

Real-time payments are available around the clock and have no cut-off. See the *Juniper Bay Payments Real-Time Rail Service Guide*.

## Holidays

Wires and EFT files are not processed on Canadian bank holidays or weekends. Instructions received on those days are processed on the next business day. International wires may also be delayed by holidays in the destination country.

## Holds and reviews

Some payments are held for review before release. A hold can delay a payment past its cut-off. Juniper Bay Payments does not publish review criteria, and holds are not a refusal of service. If a payment is held, a client will be contacted through the contact details on file.

## Questions and complaints

Clients with questions about a fee should contact their relationship manager. Complaints follow the process in the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*, and a summary of that process is given to clients on request.

## Changes to this schedule

Juniper Bay Payments gives clients at least 60 days' notice before increasing a fee. Cut-off times may change with shorter notice if a payment network changes its own operating hours.
