# High-Net-Worth Client Segmentation Thresholds

Owner: Head of Wealth
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Confidential
Version: 1.5, Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This document defines how Juniper Bay Wealth assigns clients to service segments, including the high-net-worth segment threshold. It is the authority behind the segment flag used in the *Wealth KYC Refresh Procedure* and in service-model decisions. All figures are fictional and deliberately round.

## Measure

A client's segment is based on household investable assets held with Juniper Bay Wealth, in CAD. Household means the client, their spouse or partner and dependent children who hold accounts with us. Investable assets include cash, securities and funds, and exclude real estate, private-company shares and pensions that cannot be accessed.

## Segments

| Segment | Household investable assets (CAD) | Service model |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Below 250,000 | Advisor team, annual review |
| Premier | 250,000 to under 1,000,000 | Dedicated advisor, annual review plus mid-year check-in |
| High-net-worth | 1,000,000 to under 5,000,000 | Senior advisor, semi-annual review, tax and estate planning access |
| Ultra-high-net-worth | 5,000,000 and above | Private client team, quarterly review, family-office services |

The high-net-worth client threshold is therefore CAD 1,000,000 in household investable assets. A client becomes high-net-worth when assets are at or above the threshold on the last business day of two consecutive months.

## Movement between segments

- **Upgrade.** Takes effect on the first business day of the month after the second month-end at or above the threshold.
- **Downgrade.** A client moves down only after assets are below the threshold for six consecutive month-ends. A drop of more than 10% below the threshold triggers a review by the Regional Director, Wealth.
- Segment changes are made by Wealth Operations, not by advisors.

## Effects on controls

- High-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients are refreshed at least annually under the *Wealth KYC Refresh Procedure*.
- Suitability exceptions for these clients follow the limits in the *Suitability Exception Register Policy*.
- Advisor book assignments and bonus calculations use segment counts, as described in the *Advisor Compensation Grid*.

## Handling

Segment thresholds are not shared with clients or with staff outside Wealth. Advisors may tell a client which service model applies, but not the dollar boundaries. Handling follows the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*.

## Review

The Head of Wealth reviews the thresholds each January and records any change here as a new version.
