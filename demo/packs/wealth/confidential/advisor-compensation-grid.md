# Advisor Compensation Grid

Owner: Head of Wealth
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Confidential
Version: 2.0, Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This grid explains how advisor compensation is calculated at Juniper Bay Wealth, including the advisor bonus tiers. It is the document behind the references in *Wealth Complaints Handling*. All figures are fictional.

## Structure

An advisor's total pay has three parts: base salary, a variable bonus and any recognition awards. This grid covers the bonus.

## Bonus scorecard

The bonus is calculated annually on the advisor's book of business, using a scorecard with three measures:

| Measure | Weight |
|---|---|
| Net new assets | 40% |
| Client retention and growth in book | 30% |
| Quality and conduct | 30% |

## Advisor bonus tiers

The bonus is a percentage of base salary, set by the weighted scorecard result.

| Scorecard result | Bonus tier | Bonus (% of base salary) |
|---|---|---|
| Below 60 | Below threshold | 0% |
| 60 to 74 | Tier 1 | 10% |
| 75 to 89 | Tier 2 | 20% |
| 90 and above | Tier 3 | 30% |

Net new assets are measured in CAD and each advisor has a target set at the start of the year. Assets of high-net-worth clients count at 1.25 times their value; the segmentation is set by the *High-Net-Worth Client Segmentation Thresholds*.

## Quality and conduct gates

The conduct measure is a gate as well as a weight.

- An upheld complaint about advice or conduct in the year reduces the advisor's bonus tier by one tier.
- Two or more upheld complaints make the advisor ineligible for a bonus that year.
- A confirmed suitability breach under the *Suitability Exception Register Policy* makes the advisor ineligible for the bonus.
- Overdue KYC refreshes above 5% of the book under the *Wealth KYC Refresh Procedure* reduce the conduct score by 10 points.

Complaint outcomes come from the process in *Wealth Complaints Handling*. A complaint that is not upheld has no effect.

## Payment and clawback

Bonuses are paid in March for the previous year. Fifty percent is paid in cash and fifty percent is deferred for twelve months. The deferred amount is forfeited if a serious conduct finding is later made about the year in question.

## Governance

The grid is approved annually by the Head of Wealth and the group Human Resources function. Individual results are confidential between the advisor, their manager and Human Resources.
