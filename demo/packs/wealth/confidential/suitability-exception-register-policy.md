# Suitability Exception Register Policy

Owner: Wealth Compliance Officer
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Confidential
Version: 2.1, Effective: 2026-03-01

## Purpose

This policy sets out who may approve a suitability exception at Juniper Bay Wealth, the value limits for each approver, and how exceptions are recorded in the suitability exception register. It supplies the specifics that the *Wealth Suitability Review Procedure* leaves out. All figures are fictional.

## Definition

A suitability exception is a recommendation or client-directed order that does not match the client's documented risk profile, objectives or concentration limits but is allowed for a documented reason.

## Approval limits

The approver for a suitability exception depends on the value of the exception order, measured as the amount invested outside the client's profile.

| Exception value (CAD) | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 50,000 | Branch Manager |
| 50,001 to 250,000 | Regional Director, Wealth |
| 250,001 to 1,000,000 | Wealth Compliance Officer |
| Above 1,000,000 | Head of Wealth together with the Wealth Compliance Officer |

The exception approval limit applies to a client's combined open exceptions, not each order alone. If a new exception takes the client's combined open value above the next band, the higher approver must approve it.

An advisor may never approve an exception for their own client. A manager may not approve an exception that benefits a relative or a person with whom they have a conflict.

## Conditions

An exception can only be approved if all of the following hold:

1. The client has confirmed in writing that they understand the risk.
2. The client's total exception holdings stay below 25% of their investable assets.
3. The reason is documented and specific. "Client insisted" is not enough.
4. The exception is not for a client flagged as vulnerable. Those cases go straight to the Wealth Compliance Officer.

## Maximum duration

An exception remains valid for 12 months. It must then be reviewed and either closed or renewed with the same approver level. Exceptions that are not renewed in time are treated as breaches.

## The register

Each exception is logged within one business day of approval with the client reference, value, approver, reason, expiry date and review dates. The register is reviewed by the Wealth Compliance Officer each month. A summary is reported to the Head of Wealth quarterly. An exception logged more than five business days after the order is a reportable breach.

## Breaches

An order placed before approval is recorded is a breach. The advisor is notified, the Wealth Compliance Officer opens an incident and the order is reviewed for client harm.
