# Wealth KYC Refresh Procedure

Owner: Head of Wealth Operations
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Internal
Version: 2.2, Effective: 2026-03-01

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how advisors and operations staff refresh know-your-client (KYC) information for Juniper Bay Wealth clients. It applies the *Juniper Bay Group AML/ATF Programme Summary* to the wealth business and should be read with it. The group document remains the authority on programme requirements.

## Scope

All client relationships with an open account, including registered accounts, joint accounts and accounts held through a corporation or trust.

## Refresh cycle by segment

The time between refreshes depends on the client's risk rating and wealth segment:

- High-risk clients are refreshed every year.
- Medium-risk clients are refreshed every two years.
- Low-risk clients are refreshed every three years.
- Clients in the high-net-worth segment are refreshed on the cycle of their risk rating or annually, whichever is sooner.

The high-net-worth segment threshold, and the boundaries of the other segments, are set out in the *High-Net-Worth Client Segmentation Thresholds* (Confidential). This procedure does not repeat them. If you need to know whether a client is in the high-net-worth segment, check the segment flag in the client record, which operations maintains from that document. Advisors without access to the confidential document should ask Wealth Operations.

## Triggers for an early refresh

Refresh sooner than the cycle requires when:

1. the client's circumstances change materially, for example a change of employer, residence or marital status;
2. there is a large or unusual transaction that does not match the client's profile;
3. the client's risk rating is raised;
4. a client's adverse media or sanctions status changes;
5. the client asks for an increase in investment risk.

## Steps

1. Operations generates the refresh worklist on the first business day of each month and assigns each case to the advisor of record.
2. The advisor contacts the client within ten business days and confirms identity details, address, occupation, source of funds and investment objectives.
3. The advisor records the outcome in the client record and attaches any new documents.
4. Where a client's answers have changed, the advisor flags the account for a suitability review under the *Wealth Suitability Review Procedure*.
5. Operations reviews the case and closes it. Any case with unresolved discrepancies goes to the Wealth Compliance Officer.

## Overdue refreshes

A refresh that is thirty days overdue is reported to the Head of Wealth Operations. At sixty days overdue, new purchases in the account are restricted until the refresh is complete. Withdrawals are not restricted, but the Wealth Compliance Officer is notified.

## Records

Refresh records are kept for the period required by the group retention schedule. They must not be stored outside the client record. Handling follows the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*.
