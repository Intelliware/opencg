# Wealth Complaints Handling

Owner: Head of Client Experience
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Internal
Version: 1.8, Effective: 2026-02-01

## Purpose

This document applies the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard* to Juniper Bay Wealth. The group standard sets the principles, timelines and reporting; this document describes the wealth-specific steps. Where the two differ, the group standard prevails.

## What counts as a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction about our service, advice, fees, errors or conduct, made orally or in writing, is a complaint. Advisors must not decide that something is "just feedback". If in doubt, log it.

## Wealth-specific steps

1. **Log.** The person who receives the complaint logs it in the complaints system the same business day.
2. **Acknowledge.** Client Care acknowledges the complaint within the timeline in the group standard.
3. **Assign.** Client Care assigns an investigator who has no involvement in the matter. Complaints about an advisor are never investigated by that advisor or their direct manager.
4. **Investigate.** The investigator reviews the client file, the suitability record under the *Wealth Suitability Review Procedure*, call recordings and trade history.
5. **Decide and respond.** The investigator proposes an outcome and any redress. The Head of Client Experience approves.
6. **Close.** The response letter explains the outcome and the client's right to take the matter to an external dispute-resolution body.

## Complaint types that need escalation

- Allegations of unsuitable advice, unauthorised trading or misrepresentation go to the Wealth Compliance Officer on the same day.
- Complaints that suggest possible fraud or financial abuse of a vulnerable client go to the Wealth Compliance Officer immediately.
- Suspicions that a transaction may be linked to money laundering are handled under the *Juniper Bay Group AML/ATF Programme Summary* and must not be discussed with the client.

## Redress

Redress for errors is decided case by case, according to approval levels held by the Head of Client Experience. Redress must put the client back in the position they would have been in.

## Effect on advisors

The outcome of a complaint is part of an advisor's record. Upheld complaints may affect eligibility for variable pay. The conditions are defined in the *Advisor Compensation Grid* (Confidential); this document does not repeat them. Advisors who want to know how a complaint affects their bonus should speak to their manager or Wealth Compliance Officer.

## Reporting

Client Care reports complaint volumes, themes and ageing monthly to the Head of Wealth and the group complaints function.
