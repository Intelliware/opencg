# Wealth Suitability Review Procedure

Owner: Wealth Compliance Officer
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Internal
Version: 3.0, Effective: 2026-03-01

## Purpose

This procedure describes how advisors assess and document the suitability of investments for Juniper Bay Wealth clients. It reflects Juniper Bay Wealth's approach to meeting the suitability and know-your-client expectations of the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO), described here at a high level only.

## When a suitability review is required

A review is required:

- when an account is opened;
- when a recommendation to buy, sell or switch is made;
- when KYC information changes, including after a refresh under the *Wealth KYC Refresh Procedure*;
- when a portfolio's risk profile no longer matches the client's;
- at least once a year for every managed account.

## The assessment

The advisor documents:

1. the client's goals, time horizon and liquidity needs;
2. financial position, including income, assets and liabilities;
3. investment knowledge and experience;
4. risk tolerance and capacity for loss;
5. how the recommended portfolio fits each of the above and why.

The recommendation must be one of the five portfolios in the *Juniper Bay Wealth Managed Portfolio Family Overview* unless an exception is granted.

## Outcomes

A review ends in one of three outcomes:

- **Suitable.** The advisor records the rationale and proceeds.
- **Not suitable.** The advisor does not proceed and explains the reasons to the client. Alternatives are offered.
- **Suitable by exception.** The client wants an investment that does not match the profile, and there is a documented reason to allow it.

## Suitability exceptions

An exception is allowed only with written approval and is logged in the suitability exception register. The approver levels, the value limits for each level and the maximum time an exception may remain open are defined in the *Suitability Exception Register Policy* (Confidential). This procedure does not repeat them. An advisor who needs to know who can approve an exception for a given amount should ask the Wealth Compliance Officer, who can look it up.

Never place an order that needs an exception until approval is recorded.

## Escalation

If the advisor and the client disagree about suitability, or the advisor believes a client is being pressured by a third party, escalate the same day to the Wealth Compliance Officer.

## Quality assurance

Each month the Wealth Compliance Officer samples completed reviews and checks that rationale is documented and exceptions are properly approved. Results are reported to the Head of Wealth.
