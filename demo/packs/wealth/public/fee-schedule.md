# Juniper Bay Wealth Fee Schedule

Owner: Head of Investment Products
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Public
Version: 3.1, Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This schedule lists the fees charged on Juniper Bay Wealth managed portfolio accounts. All fees are illustrative figures in Canadian dollars and are shown before applicable taxes.

## Annual advisory fee

The advisory fee is charged as a percentage of the account's average daily market value, billed monthly in arrears. It covers advice, portfolio management and reporting.

| Account value (CAD) | Annual advisory fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 250,000 | 1.00% |
| 250,000 to 1,000,000 | 0.80% |
| Above 1,000,000 | 0.60% |

The tiers apply to the value within each band, not to the whole account.

## Underlying portfolio cost

Each managed portfolio also bears a management expense ratio for the funds it holds. These are listed in the portfolio's disclosure document and are in addition to the advisory fee.

| Portfolio | Illustrative underlying cost |
|---|---|
| Harbour Preservation | 0.10% |
| Harbour Income | 0.12% |
| Harbour Balanced | 0.15% |
| Harbour Growth | 0.18% |
| Harbour Equity | 0.20% |

## Other fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Account opening | None |
| Registered plan administration (per plan, per year) | CAD 100 |
| Transfer out to another institution | CAD 150 per account |
| Trade made outside the managed programme, per trade | CAD 25 |
| Paper statement, per statement | CAD 5 |
| Cheque or bank-draft withdrawal | CAD 20 |

Electronic statements and electronic transfers are free.

## Fee changes

We will give at least sixty days' written notice before increasing a fee. Fee reports are provided annually and show, in dollars, the amounts you paid.

## Questions about fees

Your advisor can walk you through how fees apply to your accounts. If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, tell your advisor or contact Client Care. Complaints are handled under the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*.

For the products these fees apply to, see the *Juniper Bay Wealth Managed Portfolio Family Overview*.

Juniper Bay Financial Group is fictional. The fees above are illustrative and are not a real offer.
