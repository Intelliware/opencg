# Juniper Bay Wealth Client Onboarding Guide

Owner: Head of Client Experience
Applies to: Juniper Bay Wealth
Classification: Public
Version: 1.4, Effective: 2026-02-01

## Welcome

This guide explains what happens when you become a client of Juniper Bay Wealth, what we ask of you and what you can expect from us.

## Step 1: Initial conversation

You meet an advisor, in person or by video. The advisor explains our services, how we are paid and the portfolios we offer, as described in the *Juniper Bay Wealth Managed Portfolio Family Overview* and the *Juniper Bay Wealth Fee Schedule*. There is no cost or obligation at this stage.

## Step 2: Tell us about yourself

Canadian rules require us to know our clients. We ask about:

- your goals and the time you have to reach them;
- your income, assets and debts;
- your investment experience and how you feel about risk;
- your tax situation and the accounts you want to open.

This is called a know-your-client profile. Your answers shape the advice you receive, so please be as accurate as you can.

## Step 3: Prove your identity

We must verify your identity before opening an account. You will need one or two pieces of government-issued photo identification. We may also ask about the source of the funds you intend to invest. These requirements come from Canadian anti-money-laundering law, and we apply them as set out in the *Juniper Bay Group AML/ATF Programme Summary*. If you hold funds for someone else, or are acting for a company or trust, tell us early so we can collect the right documents.

## Step 4: Review and sign

Your advisor recommends a portfolio and explains why it suits you. You receive the account agreement, the risk disclosure and the fee information. You decide whether to proceed. Nothing is opened until you sign.

## Step 5: Fund your account

You can transfer money electronically or move accounts from another institution. We will tell you how long each takes. Your portfolio is invested once funds arrive.

## After you are open

- You will receive a welcome pack and online access within five business days.
- Your advisor reviews your plan with you at least once a year.
- We will occasionally ask you to confirm or update your details. This is a normal part of keeping our records accurate.

## Privacy

We use your personal information to provide services, meet legal obligations and keep your accounts safe. We do not sell it. Your advisor can explain your privacy choices.

## If something goes wrong

Tell your advisor. If you are not satisfied, you can make a formal complaint, and we will handle it under the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*.

Juniper Bay Financial Group is fictional. This guide is illustrative and is not legal or regulatory guidance.
