## Purpose

This overview describes the managed portfolios offered by Juniper Bay Wealth to its clients in Canada. It is a plain-language summary. It is not an offering document and does not replace the account agreement or the fund and portfolio disclosure documents provided at account opening.

## The portfolio family

Juniper Bay Wealth offers five model-based managed portfolios. Each is built from a diversified mix of Canadian, US and international equities, fixed income and cash, and each is managed to a stated risk profile.

| Portfolio | Risk profile | Typical equity share | Suited to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harbour Preservation | Low | 20% | Clients who value stability and income |
| Harbour Income | Low to medium | 40% | Clients who want income with some growth |
| Harbour Balanced | Medium | 60% | Clients with a medium-term horizon |
| Harbour Growth | Medium to high | 80% | Clients with a long horizon who accept volatility |
| Harbour Equity | High | 100% | Clients with a very long horizon and high risk tolerance |

The equity shares above are targets. Actual holdings drift between rebalancing events and are brought back to target as described in the internal rebalancing process.

## How a portfolio is matched to a client

An advisor recommends a portfolio only after completing a know-your-client profile and a suitability assessment. The assessment considers the client's goals, time horizon, financial situation, investment knowledge and risk tolerance. A client may decline a recommendation, but the advisor will record that the recommendation was made and why the client chose otherwise.

## Reporting and review

Clients receive a quarterly statement and an annual performance and fee report. Advisors meet each client at least once a year to review the portfolio against the client's goals. Clients may ask for a review at any time.

## Account types

Managed portfolios are available in registered and non-registered accounts, including RRSP, TFSA, RESP and joint accounts, subject to eligibility. Your advisor can explain which account type fits your situation.
