# Juniper Bay Bank Dispute and Chargeback Guide

Owner: Head of Retail Card Services
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Public
Version: 1.5
Effective: 2026-03-01

This guide tells customers what to do if a card or account transaction looks wrong, and what to expect from us. Timeframes are fictional and illustrative.

## When to raise a dispute

Contact us as soon as you notice a transaction you do not recognise, were charged twice, were charged the wrong amount, or did not receive goods or services you paid for. Please raise a dispute within 60 days of the statement date on which the transaction appears. Later disputes are still reviewed, but options may be more limited.

## Before you contact us

- Check the merchant name on your statement. Some merchants trade under a different name.
- Look for receipts, order confirmations and messages with the merchant.
- Where possible, try to resolve the issue with the merchant first. You do not have to do this for suspected fraud.

## Suspected fraud

If you think your card or account details were stolen, call the number on the back of your card at once. We will block the card, issue a replacement and open an investigation. You are not responsible for fraud on your account when you have taken reasonable care of your card and PIN and have told us promptly, as set out in your cardholder agreement.

## What happens after you tell us

1. We confirm your identity and record the details of the dispute.
2. We tell you the reference number and the next steps.
3. For many disputes we place a temporary credit on the account while we investigate. The temporary credit can be reversed if the investigation finds the transaction was valid.
4. We investigate and may ask you for more information. Please reply within 10 days.
5. We tell you the outcome in writing.

Most disputes are resolved within 30 days. Complex cases, including those involving other countries, can take up to 90 days. We will keep you updated.

## What is a chargeback

A chargeback is a request, made through the card network, for the merchant's bank to return money for a transaction. It follows network rules, which set reasons, evidence and deadlines. A chargeback is not guaranteed. The merchant can respond with proof the transaction was valid, and the card network decides in some cases.

## Common cases

| Situation | What we usually need |
|---|---|
| Unrecognised transaction | Confirmation you did not make or authorise it |
| Duplicate charge | Statement showing both charges |
| Goods not received | Order details and evidence you contacted the merchant |
| Cancelled subscription still billing | Cancellation date and confirmation |
| Wrong amount | Receipt showing the correct amount |

## Fees

We do not charge a fee to investigate a dispute. If a dispute is found to be invalid, a temporary credit is reversed and any overdraft costs that result still apply.

## If you disagree with the outcome

You can ask for a second review. If you are still unhappy, you can make a complaint, and we will handle it in line with our published complaint process, with escalation to an external body for banking complaints if needed.
