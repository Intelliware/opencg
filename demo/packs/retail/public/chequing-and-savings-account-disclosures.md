# Juniper Bay Bank Chequing and Savings Account Disclosures

Owner: Head of Retail Deposit Products
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Public
Version: 3.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

This document summarises the key terms of Juniper Bay Bank personal chequing and savings accounts. It is a plain-language summary for customers. The full account agreement governs if there is any difference. Juniper Bay Bank is a member of CDIC, and eligible deposits are protected up to the limits CDIC publishes.

## Accounts at a glance

| Account | Monthly fee | Included transactions | Interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everyday Chequing | CAD 10 | 20 debit transactions | None |
| Premium Chequing | CAD 25 | Unlimited | None |
| Student Chequing | CAD 0 | Unlimited | None |
| Everyday Savings | CAD 0 | 2 withdrawals | Tiered, posted monthly |
| High Interest Savings | CAD 0 | 2 withdrawals | Higher rate, posted monthly |

Fees shown are fictional and illustrative. Extra debit transactions on Everyday Chequing cost CAD 1 each. Additional savings withdrawals cost CAD 5 each.

## Fee waivers

The monthly fee on Everyday Chequing is waived when the account holds a minimum daily balance of CAD 2,000, or when direct deposit of at least CAD 1,000 a month is set up. Premium Chequing is waived at a combined balance of CAD 25,000 across Juniper Bay Bank accounts. Customers aged 65 or over may ask a branch about a seniors fee reduction.

## Interest

Interest on savings accounts is calculated daily on the closing balance and posted on the last business day of the month. Rates are published on the Juniper Bay Bank website and may change without notice. Promotional rates state their end date.

## Deposits, holds and availability

- Cash deposited at a branch is available the same business day.
- Cheques are normally available within 4 business days. A longer hold may apply to new accounts, large cheques or cheques that appear unusual.
- Direct deposits are available on the day they are received.
- Email money transfer (e-Transfer) funds are available when the transfer is accepted.

When a hold is placed, we tell the customer the amount, the reason and the date the funds become available.

## Statements and notices

Statements are provided monthly, in electronic form by default. Paper statements are available on request. Customers should review each statement promptly and tell us about any error. Our dispute process is described in the *Juniper Bay Bank Dispute and Chargeback Guide*.

## Overdraft

Overdraft protection is optional and is described in the *Juniper Bay Bank Overdraft Protection Terms*. Without overdraft protection, a payment that would overdraw the account is declined and a non-sufficient-funds fee may apply.

## Closing an account

A customer may close an account at any time at no charge. Any fees owing and any items still in process are settled first. We may close an account with notice as set out in the account agreement.

## Identification and privacy

We verify the identity of every customer as required by Canadian law, including the PCMLTFA. We collect and use personal information only as described in the Juniper Bay Bank Privacy Notice. Customers may withdraw consent for marketing at any time.

## Complaints

If a customer is not satisfied, they can ask any branch or call the contact centre. Complaints are handled in stages with published response times. If a complaint is not resolved, the customer may escalate it to an external body for banking complaints.

## Contact

Customers can visit any Juniper Bay Bank branch, use online banking, or call the number on the back of their card.
