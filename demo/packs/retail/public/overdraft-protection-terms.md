# Juniper Bay Bank Overdraft Protection Terms

Owner: Head of Retail Deposit Products
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Public
Version: 2.2
Effective: 2026-01-01

These terms explain how overdraft protection works on Juniper Bay Bank personal chequing accounts. They supplement the *Juniper Bay Bank Chequing and Savings Account Disclosures*. All amounts are fictional and illustrative.

## What overdraft protection does

Overdraft protection lets a payment go through when the account balance is too low, up to an agreed limit. The amount used is a loan, and the customer must repay it promptly. Overdraft protection is not automatic. A customer must apply and be approved, and may remove it at any time.

## Limits

Overdraft limits are offered in steps of CAD 100, from CAD 100 up to CAD 1,000 for most customers. The limit offered depends on how long the customer has banked with us, how the account has been managed and the customer's overall relationship. Limits are reviewed from time to time and may be reduced or removed with notice.

## Costs

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly overdraft protection fee | CAD 5, charged only in months the overdraft is used |
| Interest on the overdrawn balance | Daily, at the rate published on our website |
| Non-sufficient-funds fee (no protection, item declined) | CAD 45 |
| Item paid beyond the limit | CAD 20 |

Interest accrues each day the account is overdrawn and is charged at month end.

## How items are paid

Items are processed in the order they are received during the day. On the following business day we apply credits first, then debits. If a payment would take the account past its limit, we may decline it. Debit purchases that would overdraw an account without protection are declined at the till.

## Repayment

Any deposit to the account first repays the overdrawn amount. We ask customers to bring the account back within its balance within 30 days. A customer who has trouble repaying should contact us early. Support options are described on our website, and staff can offer a conversation about repayment arrangements.

## Alerts

Customers can set balance alerts by text or email. We send an alert when an account first goes into overdraft and when the overdraft reaches 80 percent of its limit.

## Removing overdraft protection

A customer can cancel overdraft protection at a branch, by phone or in online banking. Any amount owing remains due. We may suspend or withdraw protection if the account is misused, if we suspect fraud, or if the customer's circumstances change materially.

## Questions and complaints

If you believe a fee was charged in error, tell us and we will review it. We handle complaints in line with our published process. For disputes about card transactions, see the *Juniper Bay Bank Dispute and Chargeback Guide*.
