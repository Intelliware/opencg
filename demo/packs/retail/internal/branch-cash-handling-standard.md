# Branch Cash Handling Standard

Owner: Head of Branch Operations
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Internal
Version: 2.4
Effective: 2026-01-15

This standard sets out how branch staff receive, store, count and move cash. It is written for tellers, head tellers and branch managers. It operates alongside the *Juniper Bay Group AML/ATF Programme Summary* for reporting and the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard* for handling records.

## Principles

- Cash is always under dual control when outside a teller drawer.
- Every movement of cash is recorded at the time it happens.
- A discrepancy is reported the same day, however small.

## Teller drawers

1. Each teller has their own drawer and signs on with their own credentials. Do not share drawers or sign-ons.
2. Count the opening balance with the head teller before the first transaction.
3. Keep only the cash needed for the next hour in the drawer. Move excess to the vault with the head teller.
4. Lock the drawer whenever you leave the counter, even briefly.
5. Count the drawer at close and reconcile to the system. Another employee witnesses the count.

## Vault and safe

The vault is opened under dual control by two authorised employees. Neither may know both halves of any combination or hold both keys. The vault log records who opened it, when and why. Time-lock settings are changed only by the branch manager and recorded.

## Counting and counterfeit detection

Count notes face-up, twice, and use the note-checking device on every deposit above a small amount. If a note looks suspect, do not return it. Keep it, give the customer a receipt for the item, and follow the counterfeit report process.

## Large cash transactions

Staff must follow the large cash reporting requirements in the *Juniper Bay Group AML/ATF Programme Summary*, which reflects obligations under the PCMLTFA and reporting to FINTRAC. Do not split or encourage splitting a transaction to avoid reporting. Record who conducted the transaction and, where required, on whose behalf.

## Cash orders and shipments

Cash orders from the cash centre are checked on arrival by two employees against the manifest. Sealed bags are not opened until both are present. Any seal damage is reported immediately and the shipment is set aside.

## Discrepancies

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Teller drawer short or over | Recount, report to head teller, log in the discrepancy register |
| Repeated discrepancies for one teller | Branch manager reviews and coaches |
| Suspected theft or fraud | Stop, secure the area, call the Branch Security Lead, do not confront |
| Vault out of balance | Notify the branch manager and the cash centre at once |

## Robbery and safety

Staff safety comes first. Follow the branch robbery procedure: comply, do not resist, trigger the silent alarm only if safe, note descriptions afterwards and keep the area clear.

## Records

Keep the discrepancy register, the vault log and cash-order records for the period set in the records schedule. Do not store images of customer identification on personal devices.
