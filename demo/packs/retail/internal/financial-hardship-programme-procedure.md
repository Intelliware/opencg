# Financial Hardship Programme Procedure

Owner: Head of Retail Customer Support
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Internal
Version: 2.1
Effective: 2026-04-01

This procedure tells Customer Support and branch staff how to help customers who are having trouble repaying a loan, credit card or overdraft. It sits alongside the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard* and depends on the *Retail Collections Strategy* (Confidential) for the stage timings and the rules for external collections. This procedure does not repeat them.

## Purpose

Customers in difficulty deserve early, respectful and practical help. The hardship programme exists to find a workable arrangement before an account becomes seriously overdue. Staff should treat a hardship conversation as a service moment, not a collection call.

## Who is eligible

A customer may ask for hardship support at any point, including before missing a payment. Common triggers include job loss, illness, a change in family circumstances or a sudden increase in costs. Staff do not need proof to start a conversation. Evidence may be requested for longer arrangements.

## The conversation

1. Listen first. Ask open questions about what has changed.
2. Record income, essential costs and other debts in the hardship workflow.
3. Explain the options in plain terms and what each one costs the customer over time.
4. Agree next steps and confirm them in writing.
5. Offer a referral to free external credit counselling.

## Options

| Option | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Short payment deferral | Temporary shock, expected recovery |
| Reduced payments for a set period | Income dropped but still positive |
| Term extension | Payment too high for the current budget |
| Interest or fee pause | Where a pause makes repayment realistic |
| Consolidation | Several debts that can be combined |

Which options can be offered depends on the product and the stage of the account. Approval levels sit with Customer Support team leads and Retail Credit Underwriting.

## When an account is overdue

Overdue accounts move through collections stages. The point at which an account moves to external collections, the contact limits at each stage and the settlement authorities are set out in the *Retail Collections Strategy* (Confidential). If you are asked by a customer or colleague when an account moves to external collections, use that document. A customer who has an agreed hardship arrangement in place and is keeping to it should not be moved forward.

## Conduct

- Contact customers only at reasonable times and by approved channels.
- Never threaten, shame or mislead a customer.
- Respect requests for communication through a representative.
- Treat vulnerability as a reason to slow down and escalate to a team lead.

## Records and complaints

Record every contact in the case. A customer who says they are unhappy is making a complaint, and it is handled under the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*. Personal financial details are handled according to the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*.

## Review

Team leads review a sample of hardship cases each month for tone, accuracy of options and documentation. Results feed the quarterly retail conduct report.
