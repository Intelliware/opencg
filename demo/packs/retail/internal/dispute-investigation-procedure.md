# Retail Dispute Investigation Procedure

Owner: Head of Retail Card Services
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Internal
Version: 3.0
Effective: 2026-03-01

This procedure is for the Card Disputes team and branch staff who take dispute reports. It is the internal counterpart of the public *Juniper Bay Bank Dispute and Chargeback Guide*. A dispute that becomes a complaint also follows the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*. Where goodwill refunds are involved, this procedure depends on the *Retail Pricing Exception Authorities* (Confidential).

## 1. Intake

1. Authenticate the customer using the approved questions.
2. Record the transaction, the date, the amount, the merchant and what the customer says went wrong.
3. Classify the case: fraud, merchant dispute, processing error or duplicate.
4. Create the case and give the customer the reference number.

For suspected fraud, block the card first and then take the details.

## 2. Temporary credit

For most disputes, place a temporary credit on the account on the day the case is opened. Do not place a temporary credit when:

- the customer has not been authenticated;
- the claim looks inconsistent with the facts we hold and has been referred to the Fraud team; or
- the same transaction has already been disputed and resolved.

Record the reason when no credit is given.

## 3. Investigation

- Check authorisation data, such as chip or PIN use, device and location.
- Look at the customer's recent history with the merchant.
- Request merchant evidence when raising a chargeback. Observe the card network's time limits.
- Ask the customer for further information once, in writing, with a 10 day deadline.

Target service levels: acknowledge in 1 business day, resolve most cases within 30 days and all cases within 90 days.

## 4. Decision

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Valid claim | Make the credit permanent, close the case, tell the customer |
| Not valid | Reverse the temporary credit with at least 5 business days' notice, explain why |
| Partly valid | Credit the valid part and explain the rest |
| Network decision pending | Keep the credit, tell the customer the timeline |

## 5. Goodwill refunds

Sometimes a claim is not valid under the rules but a refund is reasonable, for example after a bank error. Goodwill refunds and fee reversals need approval. The maximum goodwill refund each role may approve is set out in the *Retail Pricing Exception Authorities* (Confidential) and this procedure does not repeat it. Record the approver and reason in the case.

## 6. Quality and fraud trends

Team leads review a sample of closed cases weekly. Patterns, such as one merchant generating many disputes, go to the Fraud team. Data used in cases is handled under the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*.

## 7. Customer communication

Write in plain language. Say what we found, what we did and what the customer can do next. Always mention the right to a second review.
