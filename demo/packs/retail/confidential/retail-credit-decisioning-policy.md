# Retail Credit Decisioning Policy

Owner: Chief Retail Credit Officer
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Confidential
Version: 5.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

This policy sets the score cut-offs, debt-service ratio limits and referral rules used by the retail decision engine for unsecured personal credit. It is the source for the figures that the *Retail Account Opening and Credit Application Procedure* deliberately omits. All figures are fictional and illustrative. Distribution is limited to the retail credit, underwriting and branch lending roles with a need to know, under the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*.

## Scope

This policy covers personal loans, personal lines of credit and credit cards for individuals. Mortgages and small business lending are covered elsewhere.

## Credit score cut-offs

The decision engine uses the bureau credit score together with the customer's internal behaviour score.

| Bureau score | Outcome for a personal loan |
|---|---|
| Below 560 | Auto-decline |
| 560 to 639 | Refer to Retail Credit Underwriting |
| 640 to 719 | Approve if debt-service ratios and policy checks pass |
| 720 and above | Approve at the best available pricing tier if checks pass |

The personal loan auto-decline score cut-off is 560. A decline at this level is not overridden by branch staff. Credit cards use an auto-decline cut-off of 580 and lines of credit use 600.

A file with no bureau score (thin file) is always referred, never auto-declined.

## Debt-service ratios

The engine calculates two ratios using verified income.

- Gross debt service (GDS) applies to secured residential lending and is outside this policy.
- Total debt service (TDS) for unsecured credit includes all existing obligations plus the proposed payment.

The maximum TDS ratio is 42 percent for approval. Between 42 and 46 percent the file is referred. Above 46 percent the file is declined. For credit cards the proposed payment is assumed at 3 percent of the limit.

## Exposure limits

| Product | Maximum unsecured exposure per customer |
|---|---|
| Personal loan | CAD 50,000 |
| Line of credit | CAD 25,000 |
| Credit card (all cards combined) | CAD 20,000 |

A customer's total unsecured exposure with Juniper Bay Bank may not exceed CAD 75,000 without approval from the Chief Retail Credit Officer.

## Referral rules

Referral to underwriting is required when any of these apply: a score in the refer band, a TDS ratio between 42 and 46 percent, a thin file, unverified income, a recent bankruptcy or consumer proposal, or an applicant who is a Juniper Bay Bank employee or related to one. Underwriters may approve within the limits above but may not approve a file that breaches the auto-decline score cut-off.

## Overrides

Underwriting managers may approve a one-band override on score (up to 20 points) when the customer has held a deposit relationship for at least 3 years with no overdraft events in the last 12 months. Overrides are recorded with reasons. The override rate is reported monthly. If it exceeds 5 percent of decisions in any month, the Credit Risk Committee reviews the cut-offs.

## Fair treatment

Cut-offs apply equally to all applicants. Decisions never use prohibited grounds. Customers who are declined receive the decline notice and may ask for a review. Complaints follow the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*.

## Review cycle

The Credit Risk Committee reviews this policy every year and after any material change in portfolio performance. Changes take effect only after the engine is updated and tested.
