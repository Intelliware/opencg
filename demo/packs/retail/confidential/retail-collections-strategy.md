# Retail Collections Strategy

Owner: Head of Retail Collections
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Confidential
Version: 3.2
Effective: 2026-04-01

This strategy sets the stages, timings and authorities used to collect overdue retail debts. It supplies the specifics that the *Financial Hardship Programme Procedure* refers to and does not repeat. All figures are fictional and illustrative. It is classified Confidential under the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard* because it reveals internal timing and settlement positions.

## Objectives

1. Help customers return to good standing where that is realistic.
2. Reduce losses on accounts that cannot be cured.
3. Treat every customer fairly and in line with conduct requirements.

## Stages

Days past due (DPD) is counted from the first missed payment date.

| Stage | DPD | Owner | Main actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 1 to 29 | Customer Support | Reminder text and email, one phone call, hardship offer |
| Mid | 30 to 59 | Internal Collections | Up to 3 calls a week, written notice, arrangement discussion |
| Late | 60 to 89 | Internal Collections | Formal demand letter, account restrictions, final hardship review |
| Pre-agency | 90 to 119 | Collections Team Lead | Last offer, legal review of file |
| External collections | 120 or more | Approved agency | Placement with an approved collection agency |

An unsecured retail account moves to external collections at 120 days past due. The move is blocked if any of these apply: an active hardship arrangement being kept, an open complaint about the debt, a dispute not yet decided, or a customer flagged as vulnerable.

## Contact limits

Contacts must stay within these limits at every stage: no more than 3 attempts a week by phone, no calls before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m. local time, and no contact at the customer's workplace without consent. Contact stops on request when the customer names a representative.

## Settlement authorities

| Role | May approve a settlement of | Minimum recovery |
|---|---|---|
| Collections Agent | Up to CAD 1,000 outstanding | 80 percent |
| Collections Team Lead | Up to CAD 10,000 outstanding | 60 percent |
| Head of Retail Collections | Up to CAD 50,000 outstanding | 50 percent |
| Chief Retail Credit Officer | Any amount | Any |

## Write-off

Accounts are written off at 180 days past due, or earlier on bankruptcy, death or confirmed fraud. A write-off needs approval from the Head of Retail Collections up to CAD 25,000 and from the Chief Retail Credit Officer above that. Recovery efforts may continue after write-off through the agency.

## Agencies

Only agencies on the approved list may receive accounts. Agencies must follow the conduct code, report monthly and return an account on request. Fees are paid on recovery only, at rates held by Procurement. Agency performance is reviewed each quarter.

## Monitoring

The Retail Collections dashboard tracks roll rates, cure rates, complaints per 1,000 accounts and contact-limit breaches. A breach is investigated within 5 business days. Material trends go to the Credit Risk Committee.
