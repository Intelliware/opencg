# Retail Pricing Exception Authorities

Owner: Head of Retail Pricing
Applies to: Juniper Bay Bank
Classification: Confidential
Version: 2.3
Effective: 2026-02-01

This document sets out who may approve exceptions to published retail pricing, and how far. It supplies the figures that the *Retail Account Opening and Credit Application Procedure* and the *Retail Dispute Investigation Procedure* refer to by title and do not repeat. All figures are fictional and illustrative. Handling follows the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*.

## Principles

- An exception must be recorded in the workflow, with the reason, before it is promised to the customer.
- An approver may not approve an exception for themselves, a relative or a close associate.
- Exceptions are for relationship or service reasons, never as a way around credit policy. Credit cut-offs are in the *Retail Credit Decisioning Policy*.

## Rate discounts on advertised loan and line of credit rates

| Role | Maximum rate discount |
|---|---|
| Branch Advisor | 0.00 percentage points (no discretion) |
| Branch Manager | Up to 0.25 percentage points |
| District Manager | Up to 0.50 percentage points |
| Head of Retail Pricing | Up to 1.00 percentage points |
| Retail Pricing Committee | Above 1.00 percentage points |

A branch manager may approve a rate discount of up to 0.25 percentage points off the advertised rate. A discount beyond that goes to the District Manager. Discounts apply for the original term only and are not repeated on renewal without a new approval. The total discount cannot take the rate below the funding floor held by Treasury.

## Monthly fee waivers

| Role | Authority |
|---|---|
| Branch Advisor | Waive up to 3 months of a monthly account fee |
| Branch Manager | Waive up to 12 months |
| District Manager | Waive up to 24 months or permanently for a documented reason |

## Goodwill refunds and fee reversals

| Role | Maximum goodwill refund per case |
|---|---|
| Branch Advisor | CAD 50 |
| Card Disputes Agent | CAD 100 |
| Branch Manager | CAD 250 |
| Card Disputes Team Lead | CAD 500 |
| Head of Retail Card Services | CAD 2,500 |

The maximum goodwill refund a Card Disputes Team Lead may approve is CAD 500 per case. A customer may receive no more than CAD 1,000 of goodwill refunds in any rolling 12 months without approval from the Head of Retail Card Services. Refunds above the authority of the dispute handler are escalated within one business day.

## Overdraft and non-sufficient-funds fee reversals

Branch Advisors may reverse one non-sufficient-funds fee or overdraft fee per customer per 12 months. Branch Managers may reverse up to three. Repeated reversals trigger a review of the customer's account for hardship, following the *Financial Hardship Programme Procedure*.

## Reporting and audit

Every exception is logged. The Head of Retail Pricing reviews exception volumes by branch and approver each month. An approver who regularly exceeds peers is reviewed, and exceptions made outside authority are escalated to Compliance. Authorities are reviewed annually and take effect once published to the workflow.
