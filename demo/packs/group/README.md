# Group shared standards

Juniper Bay Financial Group is fictional. Its people, figures and documents are illustrative and are not legal or regulatory guidance.

Owner: openCG maintainers

## What this folder is

The group folder holds standards shared by all of Juniper Bay Financial Group: Juniper Bay Wealth, Juniper Bay Payments and Juniper Bay Bank. The wealth, payments and retail packs refer to these documents by title. The folder is ingested as `internal` alongside every pack, so any signed-in staff member can retrieve them. It has no `questions.json`, and no public or confidential folders.

## Documents

Internal:

- `internal/aml-atf-programme-summary.md`: *Juniper Bay Group AML/ATF Programme Summary*
- `internal/complaint-handling-standard.md`: *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard*
- `internal/data-classification-standard.md`: *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*

## Cross-pack use

A question such as "How long do we have to acknowledge a client complaint, and does that differ between wealth and retail?" is answered from the *Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard* regardless of which pack persona asks.
