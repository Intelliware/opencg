# Juniper Bay Group Complaint-Handling Standard

Owner: Chief Compliance Officer
Applies to: Juniper Bay Financial Group (Wealth, Payments, Bank)
Classification: Internal
Version: 2.1, Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This standard sets one way of handling client complaints across Juniper Bay Wealth, Juniper Bay Payments and Juniper Bay Bank. It sets out stages, timelines and record keeping. Subsidiaries may add stricter steps for their own regulators, such as CIRO for wealth dealer activity, but may not shorten what is described here. It is illustrative and is not legal or regulatory guidance.

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, staff member or fee, made verbally or in writing, where the client expects a response or remedy. If in doubt, treat it as a complaint. Staff must never discourage a client from complaining.

## Stages and timelines

Business days are used throughout.

1. **Receive and log (day 0).** The first person who hears the complaint logs it in the complaints register the same day. The client receives a written acknowledgement within 2 business days.
2. **Frontline resolution (up to 5 business days).** The team that received the complaint tries to resolve it. Resolved complaints are closed with a short written confirmation.
3. **Formal review (up to 30 calendar days from receipt).** Unresolved complaints go to the subsidiary's Complaints Officer, who investigates independently of the staff involved and sends a final written response. If the review will take longer than 30 days, the client is told the reason and given a new date, which must not be later than 60 calendar days from receipt.
4. **Senior review (up to 15 business days).** The client may ask for a senior review of the final response by the subsidiary's Head of Client Care.
5. **External escalation.** If the client is still dissatisfied, or if a final response has not been sent after 60 calendar days, the client is told how to take the complaint to the external complaints body: the independent ombudservice that serves their sector. The final response must include its name, contact details and the client's right to use it at no cost.

## Severity

Complaints are rated Standard, Serious or Critical. Serious complaints (for example alleged financial harm over CAD 10,000) are reported to the Chief Compliance Officer within 2 business days. Critical complaints (for example alleged fraud by staff, or privacy breach) are reported the same day, and the Chief Compliance Officer decides on further notifications.

## Roles

- **Frontline staff:** log, acknowledge, resolve where possible.
- **Complaints Officer (per subsidiary):** formal review and final response.
- **Head of Client Care (per subsidiary):** senior review.
- **Chief Compliance Officer:** owns this standard, receives monthly complaints reports and reports trends to the Board Risk Committee each quarter.

## Record keeping

Every complaint record contains the client reference (never full account details in free text), dates of receipt and acknowledgement, a summary, the outcome, any remedy paid and the staff who handled it. Records are kept for at least 7 years after closure. Complaint records are classified Confidential to the line of business concerned under the *Juniper Bay Group Data Classification Standard*. Complaint themes are anonymised and shared across the group as Internal.

## Root cause and learning

Each quarter, every subsidiary reviews its complaints for root causes and records actions in the complaints register. Repeated causes are escalated to the Chief Compliance Officer.
